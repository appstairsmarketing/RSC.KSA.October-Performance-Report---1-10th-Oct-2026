Analysis

Performance Notes

Key observations & recommendations · 1 Oct – 10 Oct 2026 vs Sep 14-20 2026

Booking results stepped up: 24 bookings at 155.86 SAR each (from 332.53)
Cost per booking fell 53.1% and the reported booking rate rose to 22.4% (from 5.58%). Part of that gap is definitional — last period "reached" counted any lead with feedback logged; on that earlier basis this period is about 11.3% (24 / 212) — still a clear improvement. Lead volume is lower (245 → 210) over a longer window, as spend was pulled back.
Facebook delivered 16 of the 24 bookings
Cost per booking improved to 119.25 SAR (from 155.09) with a 21.3% booking rate. Cost per lead rose 23.8% (9.94 → 12.31 SAR) and native leads eased to 148 (from 156) despite 3 more days of delivery.
Snapchat: 6 bookings at 222.02 SAR each (from 3 at 597.50)
Booking rate reached 37.5%, the best of the three platforms. Contact rate eased to 16 of 28 leads reached (from 84%). Spend dropped about half (1,792.50 → 1,097.21 SAR) even over a longer window.
Snapchat: scaling up
With a 37.5% booking rate, Snapchat is our strongest converter, so we need to increase its budget. Next step: raise the daily budget on the LASIK offer and start running Dr. Ahmed Gamal videos.
Instagram: 2 bookings after a zero last period, with lower cost per lead
Cost per lead fell 31.3% (25.11 → 17.26 SAR) and CTR eased (1.40% → 0.96%) as budget was scaled back and the content shifted to doctor videos without offers or pricing. Both bookings came from Dr. Tamer's video; with only 2 bookings it is too early to call a trend.
Doctor videos drove most of the bookings
16 of the 24 bookings came from doctor videos (Dr. Ahmed Gamal 9, Dr. Kareem 5, Dr. Tamer 2), and 8 came from the 1,750 SAR per eye LASIK offer.
TikTok: reach and views fell by about half, follows tripled
Reach 64,670 → 31,413 and video views 100,060 → 42,844, with Follow delivering 124 follows (from 40). The Video Views campaign shows a "Review issue & Appealed" status in the export and is worth checking. No leads or bookings are tracked on TikTok.
No Google Ads spend in these 10 days
We did not spend on Google between 1–10 Oct because the account needed Google Ads verification, so Google is not part of this report.
